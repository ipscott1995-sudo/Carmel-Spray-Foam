Wall Spray Foam Insulation in Carmel

Open wall cavities during construction or remodeling create an opportunity to improve both insulation and air sealing before drywall is installed. Spray foam expands after application, allowing it to conform to the shape of appropriate wall cavities and penetrations.

We provide spray foam insulation for residential wall assemblies in additions, renovations, remodeling projects, and other situations where cavities are accessible. The appropriate foam and installation method depend on the wall assembly and project requirements.

Frequently Asked Questions About Wall Spray Foam Insulation

Can spray foam be installed in existing walls?

It depends on access and the wall assembly. Spray foam is particularly practical when wall cavities are already open during a renovation, addition, or remodeling project.

Can spray foam be installed before new drywall?

Yes. Open framing before drywall installation is a common stage for installing insulation in wall cavities.

Can you insulate an addition with spray foam?

Yes. Residential additions are one of the applications where spray foam may be considered for walls, roof assemblies, and other appropriate areas.
