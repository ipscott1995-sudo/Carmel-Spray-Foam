Carmel Spray Foam Insulation FAQs

What areas of a home can be insulated with spray foam?

Depending on the construction and project requirements, spray foam may be used in attics, roof assemblies, crawl spaces, walls, rim joists, additions, and other appropriate areas.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. Call (463) 223-3884 or submit the estimate form to tell us about your insulation project.

Can spray foam be installed in an existing home?

Yes. Many spray foam projects involve existing homes, particularly attics, crawl spaces, renovations, additions, and areas where framing is accessible.

Does existing insulation need to be removed?

It depends on the project. Existing material, moisture conditions, the assembly being insulated, and the proposed installation should be evaluated first.

Can you insulate a home addition or renovation?

Yes. Additions and renovations can be good opportunities to install insulation while wall, ceiling, or roof assemblies are accessible.
