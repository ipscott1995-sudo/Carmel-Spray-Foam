Crawl Space Spray Foam in Carmel

Crawl spaces can influence the temperature and air conditions in the rooms above them. Open gaps and poorly insulated assemblies may allow outside air to move through areas beneath the home.

Spray foam can be used as part of an appropriate crawl space insulation strategy to insulate and air seal selected surfaces. Before installation, the crawl space should be evaluated for moisture, existing insulation, access, and other conditions that may need attention.

Frequently Asked Questions About Crawl Space Spray Foam

Can spray foam be used in an existing crawl space?

Yes, in many homes. The crawl space should first be evaluated for moisture, access, existing insulation, and the construction of the home.

Should moisture problems be fixed before installing spray foam?

Yes. Active leaks or other moisture problems should be addressed before insulation is installed.

Do you remove old crawl space insulation?

Existing insulation can be evaluated as part of the project to determine whether removal is necessary before new material is installed.
