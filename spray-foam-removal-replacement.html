Spray Foam Removal & Replacement in Carmel

Existing spray foam may occasionally need to be evaluated because of renovation work, damage, access requirements, or concerns with a previous installation. The appropriate solution depends on why the material needs attention and where it is installed.

We can evaluate the affected area and discuss whether localized removal, additional work, or replacement is appropriate. Because spray foam adheres to the surfaces where it is applied, removal requirements can vary significantly from one project to another.

Frequently Asked Questions About Spray Foam Removal & Replacement

Can old spray foam insulation be removed?

Yes, although the difficulty depends on the type of foam, where it was installed, and the surfaces to which it has bonded.

Does all existing spray foam need to be removed?

Not necessarily. The affected area and reason for removal should be evaluated before determining how much material needs to come out.

Can removed spray foam be replaced?

In appropriate areas, replacement insulation can be installed after the underlying issue or project work has been addressed.
